## Standard refund timing

Once Northwind Goods receives an approved return, refunds to the original payment method are normally posted within **5–7 business days**. The timing starts when the returned item is received, not when the customer hands the parcel to the carrier.

Customers should allow the full processing period before requesting a status update unless Support has another reason to investigate.

## Payment-method exceptions

Some refund methods follow different timing:

- **Gift card refunds are instant** once the refund is approved.
- **Bank transfer refunds can take up to 10 business days** after the return is received.
- Refunds to the original payment method generally remain within the standard 5–7 business-day window.

A refund approval and a refund being visible in a customer's account are separate events. Support should not describe an approved refund as already received unless the payment has actually posted.

## Examples

A $35.50 order that has already been refunded to its original payment method is complete from a refund-processing perspective, even if the customer is asking when the original return was processed.

For unusual payment situations, confirm the payment method before quoting a timeframe.
