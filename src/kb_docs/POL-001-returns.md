## Standard returns

Northwind Goods accepts returns of eligible items within **30 days of delivery**. The return window begins on the date the carrier records delivery, not the date the order was placed.

Items must be in resalable condition and include the original order information. Customers are responsible for following the return instructions provided by Support.

## Exceptions

The following rules apply even when an item is otherwise within the standard return window:

- **Final-sale items are not returnable.**
- **Electronics must be returned within 14 days of delivery**, rather than 30 days.
- **Opened hygiene items are not returnable.**
- Items that are damaged after delivery through customer handling may be subject to the damaged-item and warranty policies.

If an item is eligible, Support can provide return instructions and the applicable refund will be handled under the Refund Timelines policy.

## Return review

Returns are reviewed after they arrive at our facility. A return request made within the applicable window does not guarantee approval if the item falls under an exception above.

When a customer's circumstances involve another policy with a different rule, Support should check the relevant policy before promising an outcome.
