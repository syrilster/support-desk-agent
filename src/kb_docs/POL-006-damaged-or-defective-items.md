## Reporting a problem

Customers should report damaged or defective items within **7 days of delivery**. A photo showing the problem is required so the issue can be reviewed.

The report should identify the affected item and order. Support may request additional information when the photo does not clearly show the issue.

## Customer's options

When an item is confirmed as damaged or defective under this policy, the customer may choose between:

- A **replacement**, subject to availability; or
- A **refund**.

Support should not automatically substitute one remedy for the customer's stated preference unless the requested option is unavailable.

## Minor cosmetic damage

For minor cosmetic damage where the customer prefers to keep the item, Support may offer a **partial refund of up to 50%** of the item's applicable purchase price.

The partial-refund option is discretionary and is intended for minor cosmetic issues where the customer keeps the product. It does not change the seven-day reporting requirement.

For example, a **$150 delivered order** that receives a **$50 partial refund** has received a partial refund of about 33.3%, which is within the policy's maximum of 50%.

Cases involving safety concerns or suspected misuse should be handled under the applicable warranty and escalation guidance.
