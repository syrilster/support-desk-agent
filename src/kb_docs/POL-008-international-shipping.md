## Availability

Northwind Goods offers international shipping to **30 countries**. Delivery to eligible international destinations is estimated at **7–14 business days** after shipment.

International delivery estimates are estimates rather than guaranteed arrival dates.

## Duties and charges

The customer is responsible for applicable **customs duties and import charges** associated with an international shipment. These charges are separate from the shipping charge collected by Northwind Goods.

Customers should consider destination-specific customs processing when planning around the 7–14 business-day estimate.

## Restricted international items

Some products cannot be shipped internationally:

- **Batteries cannot be shipped internationally.**
- **Liquids cannot be shipped internationally.**

This restriction applies even when the destination is one of the 30 countries otherwise served by Northwind Goods.

Support should check the contents of the order before confirming that an international shipment can be sent. If a shipment contains a restricted battery or liquid, do not promise international dispatch for that item.

The international shipping policy concerns cross-border delivery. Domestic shipping rates and the standard domestic 3–5 business-day estimate are covered separately under the Standard Shipping policy.
