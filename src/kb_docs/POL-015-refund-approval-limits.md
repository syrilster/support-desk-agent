## Agent approval limits

Support agents may approve refunds of **up to $100** without additional approval.

Refunds from **$100.01 through $500** require **supervisor approval**.

Refunds **over $500** require **manager approval**.

The approval level is based on the refund amount being requested. Support should not split a single refund request into smaller amounts to avoid an approval requirement.

## Fraud exception

Accounts flagged for suspected fraud are handled differently. **Fraud-flagged accounts always go to the fraud team**, regardless of the refund amount.

The fraud exception therefore takes priority over the ordinary dollar-based approval path.

## Examples

A $75 refund can be approved by the agent when no other escalation trigger applies.

A $300 refund requires supervisor approval.

A $500 refund requires supervisor approval because it falls within the **$100.01–$500** band.

A $500.01 refund requires manager approval.

If an account is fraud-flagged, none of those ordinary approval limits authorize the agent, supervisor, or manager to approve the refund through the standard path; the case must go to the fraud team.
