## Customer requests

Customers may request either a **data export** or **data deletion**.

Northwind Goods will fulfill an eligible request within **30 days**. Support should record the request and follow the applicable verification process before releasing or deleting customer information.

## Retention exception

A deletion request does not remove every record immediately. **Order records must be retained for 7 years for tax purposes**, even after a customer submits a deletion request.

The retained order records should not be described as deleted when they remain subject to the seven-year retention requirement.

## Data export

For an approved data-export request, Support should follow the established process for preparing the customer's available data. The 30-day fulfillment period applies to the request as a whole.

## Data deletion

For an approved deletion request, customer information eligible for deletion should be handled through the deletion process. Records covered by the seven-year order-retention requirement remain retained for that period.

Customers should not be promised that a deletion request will erase retained order records before the seven-year period ends.

Privacy requests are separate from ordinary order support. If a request also involves account verification, fraud, or another escalation trigger, Support should follow those requirements as well.
