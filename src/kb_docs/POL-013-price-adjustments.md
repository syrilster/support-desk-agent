## Eligibility

A customer may request a **one-time price adjustment** when the price of the same eligible item drops within **14 days of purchase**.

The request must be made within the 14-day window. Support should confirm the purchase date and the later price before approving an adjustment.

## Exclusions

Price adjustments are not available for:

- **Clearance items**
- **Final-sale items**

The exclusion applies even when the price change occurs within 14 days.

## One-time rule

Each eligible purchase may receive only **one price adjustment**. A customer who has already received an adjustment for that purchase cannot receive another adjustment because the price falls again.

The adjustment concerns the qualifying price difference, subject to the item's eligibility and the applicable transaction records.

## Support review

Support should verify that the item is the same product, the purchase is within the 14-day period, and the item is not clearance or final sale.

Price adjustments are separate from returns and refunds. A customer asking for a price adjustment should not be told to return the product simply because its price later changed.

When the customer has already received a refund or other adjustment, review the transaction history before offering another adjustment.
