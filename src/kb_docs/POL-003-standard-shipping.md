## Standard rate

Northwind Goods offers standard shipping for **$5.99** on orders of **$50 or less**. Orders with a merchandise total **over $50** qualify for free standard shipping.

Standard delivery is estimated at **3–5 business days** after the order has shipped. Estimates are not a guarantee of delivery on a particular date.

## Oversized items

Oversized items use a separate shipping rate regardless of the order total:

- Oversized shipping is **$19.99**.
- The free-shipping threshold does not remove the oversized charge.
- An order containing an oversized item may therefore have a shipping charge even when its merchandise total is over $50.

The applicable rate is determined from the items and shipping method on the order.

## Delivery timing

Business-day estimates exclude weekends and should be interpreted from the shipment date. If an order is materially late, customers may have additional options under the Delayed Orders policy.

Support should quote the shipping charge and delivery estimate separately. A free standard-shipping threshold does not mean every item ships free, particularly when an oversized-item rate applies.
