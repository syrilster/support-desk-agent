## When an order is late

Northwind Goods uses the carrier's delivery estimate to determine whether an order is delayed. If an order is **more than 3 business days past its estimated delivery date**, the customer may request a refund of the shipping fee.

If an order is **more than 10 days past its estimated delivery date**, the customer may cancel the order for a **full refund**.

These thresholds are measured using business days and apply to the delivery estimate recorded for the order.

## Weather exception

Orders delayed because of **carrier weather events are excluded from the shipping-fee refund** described above. A weather-related delay does not qualify for that particular shipping-fee remedy.

Customers may still contact Support about the status of a weather-delayed order.

## Example

An order shipped on **September 27, 2026**, with an estimated delivery date of **October 2, 2026**, is five calendar days past its ETA on October 7. Support should calculate the applicable business-day delay using the order's delivery estimate and business-day calendar before approving a shipping-fee refund.

The more-than-10-business-day cancellation option is a separate threshold and should not be offered merely because the order has passed its ETA by a few days.
