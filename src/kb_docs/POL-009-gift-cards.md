## General rules

Northwind Goods gift cards **never expire**. Customers can retain an unused balance without losing it because of time.

Gift cards are **non-refundable**. A customer cannot normally exchange an issued gift card for cash or request a refund of the gift card's remaining balance.

Gift cards also **cannot be used to purchase other gift cards**. This restriction applies regardless of the value of the gift card being purchased.

## Fraud exception

A gift card that was **purchased fraudulently may be voided**.

The fraud exception is different from an ordinary refund request. Support should not promise that a fraud-related gift card will remain usable while the transaction is being investigated.

## Using gift cards

Customers should use the gift card according to the payment options presented at checkout. The no-expiration rule applies to valid gift cards and does not prevent Northwind Goods from taking action on cards identified as fraudulently purchased.

Questions about a refund issued to a gift card should also be considered alongside the Refund Timelines policy. Once an approved refund is issued to a gift card, that refund is available instantly under the refund timing rules.
