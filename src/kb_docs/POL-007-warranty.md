## Coverage period

Northwind Goods provides a **12-month limited warranty on electronics**. Products that are not electronics have a **90-day limited warranty**.

The coverage period is measured from the applicable purchase or delivery record used to establish the warranty start date.

## What the warranty covers

The limited warranty is intended for defects arising during normal use within the applicable coverage period. Customers should provide order information and a description of the problem so Support can determine whether the issue falls within warranty coverage.

Warranty coverage does not replace the separate return window. A customer may be outside the ordinary return period while still within an applicable warranty period.

## Exclusions

The warranty does **not cover water damage or misuse**.

Examples of misuse can include using a product in a way inconsistent with its intended operation or deliberately altering it. Support should not promise warranty coverage when the available information indicates water damage or misuse.

When a warranty issue also involves a safety concern, suspected fraud, or another escalation trigger, follow the Escalation Guidelines.

The warranty is limited to the periods and exclusions stated here. Support should quote the correct period based on whether the product is an electronic item or another type of product.
