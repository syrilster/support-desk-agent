## Earning points

Northwind Goods loyalty members earn **1 point for each $1 spent** on eligible merchandise.

Points are not earned on **shipping charges or taxes**. The qualifying spend is therefore based on eligible merchandise rather than the total amount paid at checkout.

## Redeeming points

**100 points equal $1 of credit.** Customers can accumulate points and redeem them according to the available loyalty redemption options.

For example, 500 eligible dollars of merchandise spend earns 500 points, which corresponds to **$5 of credit**.

## Expiration

Points expire after **12 months of inactivity**. Activity that qualifies under the loyalty program resets the applicable inactivity period.

Customers should not assume that unused points remain available indefinitely. Support should check the account's activity when a customer asks why points are no longer available.

## Important exclusions

Shipping and taxes do not generate points. A customer who pays $10 in shipping and $5 in taxes on an otherwise eligible $100 merchandise purchase earns points on the eligible merchandise amount, not on the additional $15.

The loyalty program is separate from gift cards, refunds, and price adjustments. A refund or other transaction adjustment may affect the eligible spend recorded for loyalty purposes.
