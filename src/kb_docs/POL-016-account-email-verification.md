## Identity verification

Before sharing order details, Support agents must verify the customer's identity using the **account email**.

The verification step should occur before disclosing information about orders, including order status or other order-specific details. If the customer cannot verify the account email, Support should not disclose the requested order information.

## Payment information

Northwind Goods does not share **full payment card numbers** with customers through Support.

When payment-card information needs to be referenced, Support may disclose **only the last 4 digits**.

Agents should avoid repeating sensitive payment information in ordinary messages or notes when it is not necessary to resolve the customer's request.

## Applying the rule

Identity verification is separate from refund approval, returns, and shipping policies. Even when an agent knows that an order qualifies for a refund or return, the agent must still verify the account email before sharing order details.

If the customer's request raises a suspected-fraud issue, follow the fraud and escalation requirements in addition to the identity-verification requirements.

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that order information is shared only after the customer has demonstrated control of the account email associated with the account.
