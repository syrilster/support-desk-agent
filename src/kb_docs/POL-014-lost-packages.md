## Delivered but not received

When carrier tracking shows an order as **delivered** but the customer says it was not received, the customer must first wait **48 hours** after the delivery scan.

After the 48-hour waiting period, Support may file a package claim.

## Investigation

Once a claim is filed, the investigation takes **5 business days**.

Northwind Goods does not provide a replacement or refund solely because the tracking page says delivered and the customer reports non-receipt. A replacement or refund is available **only after the investigation** is complete.

Support should explain the investigation period and avoid promising an outcome before the investigation has concluded.

## Timing

The 48-hour waiting period and five-business-day investigation are separate steps:

1. Wait 48 hours after the delivered scan.
2. File the claim.
3. Allow 5 business days for the investigation.
4. After the investigation, determine whether a replacement or refund is appropriate.

This process applies specifically to packages marked delivered that the customer has not received. Other delayed-order situations may be governed by the Delayed Orders policy.

Customers should retain any relevant delivery information while the investigation is underway.
